# ITIN-Friendly Banks, Credit Unions & Mission Lenders for Business Credit

## Who Lends to ITIN-Only Business Owners — Bureau Pulls, Bureau Reporting & Approval Requirements

**Prepared:** June 4, 2026
**Scope:** US **banks, credit unions, CDFIs, and nonprofit/mission loan funds** that are ITIN-friendly and offer **business loans and lines of credit** to owners holding only an ITIN (no SSN). For each: which credit bureaus they **pull from**, which they **report to**, and the **requirements for approval**.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first — the three things that shape everything

1. **SBA loans are now CLOSED to ITIN-only owners (and even green-card holders).** Effective ~**March 1, 2026** (SBA SOP 50 10 8 / 52 00 B under Executive Order 14159), **100% of all direct and indirect owners must be US citizens or US nationals.** This kills SBA 7(a), 504, **Microloan**, and **Community Advantage** for ITIN holders — and therefore any bank's or nonprofit's *SBA-funded* product. ITIN-friendly lenders serve you through their **own (non-SBA) capital**.

2. **"Accepts ITIN" almost always means "ITIN instead of SSN for the owner's personal-guarantee credit check."** It is rarely a special "ITIN-only / EIN-only" program. Nearly every bank/CU business loan requires a **personal guarantee**, which means a **hard pull of the owner's personal credit** — keyed to your ITIN. So you still need to *build* a US personal credit file under that ITIN. The lenders that get around this use **cash-flow or character-based underwriting** instead of a FICO score.

3. **Online applications usually block no-SSN applicants — you often must apply IN-BRANCH or by phone.** Confirmed for PNC and Citi; true across most big banks. ITIN underwriting decisions are typically made at the branch/relationship level and are *not published online* — which is also why so much below is marked "verify directly."

> **Bottom line:** The clearest "yes" for an ITIN-only owner isn't a big bank — it's a **CDFI loan fund** (DreamSpring, Accion Opportunity Fund, Working Solutions, MEDA), a **mission credit union** (Neighborhood Trust FCU, LES People's FCU, Point West), or a **community bank with cash-flow underwriting** (Beneficial State, Ponce "Prosper"). Big banks (BofA, Wells, US Bank, Citi, PNC) *do* accept ITIN for the owner's guarantee — but you'll need an established ITIN-based personal credit file and a branch visit.

## PART 1 — How Banks & Credit Unions Treat an ITIN for Business Credit (the mechanics)

**Yes, a lender can pull your consumer credit on an ITIN.** All three consumer bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) can locate or create a file keyed to an ITIN using name + DOB + address + the ITIN. Limitations: not every lender's systems accept an ITIN; ITIN files are **thinner and more prone to "mixed/split" files**; and *the ITIN alone builds nothing* — only reported tradelines do.

**What bureaus they PULL for a business loan/LOC** — usually a blend of:
- **Owner's personal credit** (Equifax/Experian/TransUnion via SSN *or* ITIN) — heavily weighted for young/thin-file businesses and any deal with a personal guarantee.
- **Business credit** — D&B PAYDEX (1–100), Experian Business / Intelliscore Plus (1–100), Equifax Business Risk Score.
- **FICO SBSS (Small Business Scoring Service, 0–300)** — the key *blended* bank/SBA score that combines the owner's personal credit + business data + application data. For a thin business file it can score mostly off the owner's personal credit. (Note: SBA is *sunsetting* its SBSS requirement March 1, 2026, but many lenders keep using it.)
- **SBFE (Small Business Financial Exchange)** — not a bureau, but a "give-to-get" data co-op that feeds D&B, Equifax, Experian, and LexisNexis. The backbone of bank/CU business-credit reporting.

**What bureaus they REPORT TO:**
- **Business performance → business bureaus + SBFE** (most banks/CUs that own/service the debt).
- **Owner's consumer file → generally only on DEFAULT** (the personal guarantee lets them report a charged-off guaranteed debt to your ITIN-keyed consumer file). Some fintech/mission lenders report *ongoing positive* to consumer bureaus to build your credit (Accion, Grameen, Ascendus, DreamSpring) — a feature, not the norm.

**The ITIN → SSN transition (important):** If you later get an SSN, your credit history does **not** automatically move. You must (1) notify the IRS to rescind the ITIN, (2) update each creditor, and (3) ask all three bureaus *in writing* to link the ITIN history to the new SSN — otherwise your SSN starts as a near-blank file while your history sits "orphaned" under the old ITIN.

**Regulatory backdrop (2026):**
- **ITIN is a valid identifier** under the USA PATRIOT Act CIP rule and beneficial-ownership/CDD rule.
- **ECOA / Reg B:** a lender generally **cannot deny credit *solely* because of immigration status**, but *can* consider status as it relates to ability to repay/collect. (CFPB & DOJ withdrew their 2023 noncitizen-borrower joint statement in Jan 2026, leaving the base Reg B text.)
- **Executive Order 14406 (May 2026), "Restoring Integrity to America's Financial System":** doesn't ban ITIN credit, but flags ITIN use by applicants *lacking verified lawful status* as a **risk factor warranting enhanced due diligence**, and directs CFPB to let lenders weigh deportation/wage-loss as an ability-to-repay factor. **Expect more identity questions and scrutiny; rules are still being written.**

## PART 6 — Recommended Game Plan for an ITIN-Only Owner

1. **Foundation:** US LLC/Corp + **EIN** (obtainable with your ITIN) + **DUNS number** + dedicated **business bank account**. Keep your **ITIN current** (renew via Form W-7 if unused 3 years — ITINs unused in 2022–2024 expired Dec 31, 2025).
2. **Build BOTH files in parallel** — a **business** file (DUNS + vendor tradelines + a business card that reports to SBFE) and an **ITIN-keyed personal** file (secured card / credit-builder), because nearly every business loan leans on the owner's personal credit + a personal guarantee.
3. **Apply where the "yes" is documented first:** a **CDFI loan fund** (DreamSpring, Accion, Working Solutions, MEDA) or a **mission CU** (Neighborhood Trust, LES People's, Point West) — these use cash-flow/character underwriting and several *report your payments to build credit.*
4. **Use a bank for separation, not as your first stop:** once you have an ITIN-based personal file, **Bank of America / Wells Fargo / U.S. Bank** (in-branch) build *business* credit via SBFE while staying off your personal report unless you default.
5. **Find more options by designation:** Inclusiv **Juntos Avanzamos** directory for CUs; **OFN CDFI Locator** for CDFIs; **CAMEO** for California microlenders. Always **call to confirm the ITIN policy covers business loans specifically.**
6. **Skip the SBA** entirely while any owner lacks US citizenship/national status (2026 rule).

## Important Caveats & Methodology

- **Point-in-time, June 2026.** Policy is shifting fast (SBA citizenship rule, EO 14406, CFPB/DOJ changes). **Confirm everything by phone before applying.**
- **Bureau pull/report fields are mostly UNVERIFIED.** Banks and especially credit unions almost never publish which of Equifax/Experian/TransUnion (or D&B/Experian Business/Equifax Business) they pull or report to. Because most member-business loans carry a personal guarantee, **expect a hard consumer pull on the owner and reporting to the owner's consumer file primarily on default** — but verify per institution.
- **ITIN acceptance for the BUSINESS product specifically** is the most common gap: many institutions (Sunrise, Self-Help, Guadalupe, GreenState, Cobalt) have well-documented ITIN *consumer/mortgage* programs but do **not** publicly confirm ITIN *business* lending. Those are marked accordingly.
- **WebFetch was blocked (HTTP 403) on nearly every bank, CU, CDFI, and government page.** Findings rely on search-result snippets cross-referenced with reputable third parties (NerdWallet, Bankrate, LendingTree, Merchant Maverick, CUInsight, Inclusiv, ICBA, OFN) plus government/bureau sources (SBA, FinCEN, CFPB, NCUA, FICO, SBFE). Items that could not be confirmed to a primary source are marked **unverified**.
- **Highest-confidence findings:** the SBA citizenship rule; the big-bank "ITIN-for-guarantee" pattern; SBFE/SBSS mechanics; and the explicit ITIN loan funds (DreamSpring, Accion, Working Solutions, MEDA, LiftFund) and mission CUs (Neighborhood Trust, LES People's, Point West).
- **Verify directly:** all bureau pull/report cells; Camino's current product-specific ITIN acceptance (for-profit, possibly narrowing); MEDA/Fondo Adelante's open/closed status; Red River's work-visa requirement; and ITIN-business eligibility at every institution marked "unverified."
- **Not legal or financial advice.** Consult a qualified advisor for your situation.
