# ITIN-Friendly, CD-Secured Business Loans & Lines of Credit

## Banks and Credit Unions That Lend Against a Pledged CD/Deposit — and What They Require

**Prepared:** June 4, 2026
**Scope:** US **banks and credit unions** offering **CD-secured** (bank term) / **share-secured** (credit-union term) **business loans and lines of credit** that are ITIN-friendly, with **approval requirements** for each. Includes the closely related deposit-secured business cards and business credit-builder products, since for an ITIN owner they solve the same problem.

---

## Why this product matters for an ITIN owner — and one honest caveat

A **CD-secured loan** lets you pledge a certificate of deposit (or savings) as collateral; the lender places a lien on it and lends you cash. Because the loan is **fully cash-collateralized**, the lender's risk is near zero — so approval rests on **the deposit, not your credit history or an SSN**. That makes it arguably the single most reliable on-ramp to US business credit for an ITIN-only, thin-file owner: little or no credit check, no meaningful FICO hurdle, a low rate, and a path to building a credit file. At credit unions the same product is called **share-secured** or **share-certificate-secured**.

**Typical terms (industry baseline):**
- **Borrow up to 90–100%** of the pledged CD/share balance (credit unions often lend the full balance).
- **Rate = the CD/share rate + ~2–3 percentage points.** Your *net* cost is just that spread, because the CD keeps earning interest while pledged.
- **Minimum deposit** can be as low as **$500–$1,000**.
- **Little/no credit check** (fully secured); the CD must stay at the lending institution and is frozen for the term.

> ⚠️ **The honest caveat (please read):** A CD-secured **personal** loan is offered almost everywhere. A CD-secured **business** product — in the business's name, reporting to *business* bureaus — is a **narrow niche**; few institutions publish one, and it's often a branch/relationship arrangement rather than a web product. This report separates the **confirmed business products** from the (far more common) **personal CD-secured loans an owner can use for the business**, and flags ITIN acceptance as "confirmed" vs. "verify at branch." We chose accuracy over a padded list.

> 🔑 **The make-or-break question to ask every lender:** *"Do you report this loan to the credit bureaus — and will you report it under my ITIN?"* The entire credit-building thesis fails if they don't. Some lenders don't report properly under an ITIN.

## PART 6 — Recommended Action Plan

1. **Open a CD / share certificate at an ITIN-friendly institution.** A **credit union with an explicit ITIN program** is the safest bet (Self-Help FCU — joinable nationwide; Point West; LES People's; Neighborhood Trust). Banks like **BMO, Citi, Ponce, BofA** accept ITIN for the deposit too.
2. **For a business product specifically:** apply to **Credit Strong Business** (explicit ITIN, reports to 4 business bureaus), **BofA Cash Secured Business LOC**, **Carver** (NYC), or **LES People's FCU**.
3. **For a pure cash-collateral loan in your name** (easiest underwriting): a **personal** CD/share-secured loan at Regions, TD, BMO, Self-Help, or Point West — up to 100% of your deposit, used for the business.
4. **Ask every lender the make-or-break questions:** Do you accept an **ITIN** to open the deposit *and* borrow? **LTV and rate** (CD rate + how many points)? Does the CD **keep earning** and the lien **release as I pay down**? **Do you report to the bureaus — under my ITIN — and to which (business vs consumer)?** Is a **personal guarantee** required?
5. **Pair** a CD/share-secured loan (installment history) with an ITIN-accepting secured card (revolving history). In ~3–6 months of on-time payments you generate a scoreable file.

## Important Caveats & Methodology

- **The product you asked about is genuinely niche.** CD-secured *personal* loans are everywhere; CD-secured *business* loans (business-named, business-bureau-reporting) are rare and often arranged at a branch. We labeled confirmed business products vs. personal products an owner can use, rather than overstating the list.
- **ITIN acceptance is firmly confirmed only where stated** — *explicitly:* Credit Strong Business, East West (secured card), and the ITIN-program credit unions/banks for *deposits* (Self-Help, Point West, LES People's, Neighborhood Trust, Latino Community CU, Sunrise, Spring Bank, Ponce, BMO, Citi). For a CD-secured *business loan* specifically, most ITIN answers are "**likely workable, confirm at branch**," because secured lending carries little credit risk but the policy is rarely published.
- **Reporting is lender-specific and must be verified** — especially that the lender reports **under your ITIN**. Confirmed business-bureau reporters here: **Credit Strong Business** (Equifax Bus/Experian Bus/PayNet/SBFE) and the secured business cards (FNBO also reports to all 3 consumer bureaus + D&B).
- **Discontinued — do not pursue:** Wells Fargo Business Secured card; MUFG Union Bank Business Secured Visa (absorbed by U.S. Bank).
- **WebFetch was blocked (HTTP 403)** on many bank/CU pages (BofA, Wells, Regions, Popular, Spring Bank, Carver, and others). Findings rely on search-result snippets cross-referenced with reputable third parties (NerdWallet, Bankrate, WalletHub, LendingTree, Nav, FitSmallBusiness, CUInsight, Inclusiv, American Banker) plus directly-readable pages. Items that couldn't be confirmed to a primary source are marked **unverified**.
- **Not legal or financial advice.** Confirm current product availability, ITIN policy, and bureau reporting directly with each institution.
