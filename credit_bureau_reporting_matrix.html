Credit Bureau Reporting Matrix

Which US Fintech & Deposit-Focused Credit Products Report — and to Which Bureaus

Prepared: June 4, 2026 Companion to: US Fintech & Deposit-Focused Companies Offering Credit Products Question answered: For each account/product, does it report payment activity to the credit bureaus, and which ones — business bureaus (Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Business, SBFE) vs. consumer bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) — and is it ongoing positive history or default-only?

How to read this — five things that matter

  1. Business bureaus ≠ consumer bureaus. Building business credit (D&B PAYDEX, Experian Business, Equifax Business) is a completely separate file from your personal credit (Equifax/Experian/TransUnion). Most fintech business products that report do so to business bureaus only.

  2. No-PG = usually nothing to your personal credit, ever. Without a personal guarantee there's no individual liable, so true no-PG corporate cards generally report nothing — positive or negative — to consumer bureaus, even on default. The generic "a missed business-card payment hits your personal credit after 60 days" warning applies to PG-backed cards, not genuine no-PG cards.

  3. SBFE is not a bureau — it's the Small Business Financial Exchange, a member data co-op that feeds D&B, Experian, Equifax, and LexisNexis. "Reports to SBFE only" means the data can surface in business reports but through a single, narrower channel.

  4. Reporting is voluntary. No law requires any issuer or vendor to report. Practices change without notice — verify before relying on any single tradeline.

  5. "Default-only" is a trap for credit-builders. A product that reports only missed payments (e.g., Uline, per several sources) can hurt you but won't build positive history. For building credit you want ongoing positive reporting.

The headline: If your goal is to build business credit with zero personal-credit exposure, the cleanest, best-documented tools are Brex, Ramp, Mercury IO, Relay, and Nav Prime (all report ongoing positive history to all three major business bureaus, no PG). For a thin personal file on an ITIN, Self is the strongest confirmed builder.

PART 6 — Strategy: Building Both Files on an ITIN

To build BUSINESS credit (no personal-credit exposure): 1. Foundation: US entity + EIN + DUNS number. 2. Open 3–4 net-30 vendors that report positive — lead with Crown Office Supplies (all 3 bureaus), add Quill and Grainger. Avoid relying on Uline for positive history. 3. Add a reporting charge card / tool: Ramp or Brex (all 3 business bureaus, no PG, no personal pull) and/or Nav Prime (monthly tradeline to all 3). 4. After 6–12 months, graduate to a reporting lender (OnDeck, Amex Business Blueprint, Bluevine) for larger limits.

To build PERSONAL credit on an ITIN: 1. Start with Self (Credit Builder + secured Visa → all 3 consumer bureaus, no credit check). 2. Add Majority secured Visa or Tilt (if ITIN accepted). 3. Once a personal score exists, a Capital One Spark card can add ongoing positive personal reporting while also funding the business.

Avoid for credit-building (they report nothing): Square Loans, Shopify Capital, PayPal Working Capital — great for cheap, credit-invisible capital, but they build no file.
