# ITIN-Friendly CDFI Banks & Loan Funds: Wyoming, California, New York & Texas

## A State-by-State Guide to Community Development Lenders for ITIN-Only Business Owners

**Prepared:** June 4, 2026
**Scope:** US Treasury–certified **CDFI banks and CDFI loan funds** (plus a few closely-related mission lenders and CDFI credit unions) that are **ITIN-friendly for business loans and lines of credit** in **Wyoming, California, New York, and Texas** — with **approval requirements** for each. Wyoming is covered first and in the most depth, because it is one of the most CDFI-thin states in the country.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first — two 2026 rules that change everything

1. **SBA loans are now closed to ITIN-only owners (and even green-card holders).** Effective ~**March–April 2026** (SBA SOP 50 10 8 and Microloan SOP 52 00 B under Executive Order 14159), **100% of all owners must be US citizens or US nationals.** This means **any SBA-funded product** — at *any* CDFI, bank, or nonprofit below — is **off-limits** to ITIN borrowers. ITIN-friendly lending must come from each lender's **own (non-SBA) capital.** Many CDFIs offer *both*; you simply route to the non-SBA product.

2. **Executive Order 14406 (May 2026)** directs banks/regulators to weigh immigration status in account opening and credit decisions and flags ITIN use by applicants lacking verified lawful status for enhanced due diligence. It doesn't ban ITIN credit, but **may chill bank (not loan-fund) ITIN lending** — which is one reason **CDFI loan funds and mission credit unions are the more reliable path** than banks for ITIN borrowers right now.

> **A note on "CDFI."** A Community Development Financial Institution is a US Treasury–certified mission lender (bank, credit union, loan fund, or venture fund). **CDFI loan funds** are nonprofits and are by far the most ITIN-flexible — many use character/cash-flow underwriting with **no minimum FICO**. Verify any certification on the **CDFI Fund list** (`cdfifund.gov`) or **OFN Locator** (`ofn.org/cdfi-locator`).

## ⭐ PART 1 — WYOMING (the priority state)

**The hard truth: Wyoming has essentially ONE Treasury-certified CDFI headquartered in the state** — the **Wind River Development Fund** (a Native CDFI loan fund). There are **no CDFI banks and no CDFI credit unions headquartered in Wyoming.** So for an ITIN-only business owner, the realistic path is **multi-state CDFIs that lend *into* Wyoming and explicitly accept an ITIN.**

> **⚠️ Myth-buster:** A search-engine/AI summary may surface **"Community Capital Wyoming"** as a WY CDFI. Our research found **no website, no CDFI Fund listing, and no independent record** of such an organization — treat it as a **fabrication** and do not rely on it.

### The Wyoming options, ranked

| Lender | CDFI? | Serves WY | ITIN for business? | Amounts | Key approval requirements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DreamSpring** ⭐ best fit | ✅ Yes (loan fund) | ✅ Statewide (Cheyenne, Casper, Laramie) | ✅ **Yes — explicit "SSN or ITIN"** | Term **$1k–$250k**; **Power Line of Credit $1k–$50k** | Flexible FICO; uncollateralized option <$20k if FICO 650+; **no open bankruptcy / collections / past-due**; 18+; use the **non-SBA** products |
| **Accion Opportunity Fund** ⭐ | ✅ Yes (loan fund) | ✅ Statewide | ✅ **Yes — accepts ITIN** | Term **$5k–$250k** | **No minimum FICO** (holistic); **≥12 months in business**; **~$50k+ annual sales**; own ≥20% |
| **MoFi** (Montana-based regional) | ✅ Yes (loan fund) | ✅ Statewide — has a **WY "Path to Bankability"** program | ⚠️ **Unverified — call to confirm** | **$1k–$1M** | Alternative/relationship underwriting for "nearly bankable" borrowers; FICO/time-in-business not published |

| **Wyoming Women's Business Center** (not a CDFI) | ❌ No | ✅ Statewide | ⚠️ Unverified (much capital is SBA → likely blocked; **USDA RMAP capital may be more flexible**) | **$500–$50k** | WY resident/operating; open to **any** owner despite the name; great **technical-assistance** resource |

**Not a fit (housing/community-facility focus, not ITIN small-business):** NewWest Community Capital, Impact Development Fund. **Ruled out:** Colorado Enterprise Fund (has a great $25k ITIN loan at 2.99% — **but Colorado only, does not lend into WY**), RMMFI (Denver only), Mountain Plains Equity Group (LIHTC housing syndicator, not a lender).

### Wyoming action plan
1. **Lead with DreamSpring** — the only lender that is *both* clearly ITIN-friendly *and* explicitly serves Wyoming, with a term loan **and** a line of credit. Use its **non-SBA** products.
2. **Apply to Accion Opportunity Fund in parallel** if you have ≥12 months operating and ~$50k+ sales.
3. **Call MoFi and Wind River Development Fund** to confirm ITIN policy — both are CDFIs actively lending in WY (WRDF if you're on/near the reservation).
4. **Use Kiva** as a 0%, ITIN-friendly supplement for smaller amounts.
5. **Treat the Wyoming Women's Business Center + Wyoming SBDC as technical-assistance** resources; skip SBA-funded products while ITIN-only.

## Important Caveats & Methodology

- **Point-in-time, June 2026.** CDFI service areas, ITIN policies, capital availability, and product terms change frequently — and 2026 policy is shifting fast. **Confirm everything by phone before applying.**
- **"CDFI certified" should be verified.** Several smaller orgs labeled CDFIs (JFLA, ICA Fund, Cal Coastal, Project Enterprise) could not be independently confirmed on the CDFI Fund list via these searches — check `cdfifund.gov`. **Amalgamated Bank and ICBC USA are NOT CDFIs** and were excluded.
- **ITIN acceptance for the BUSINESS product specifically** is the key gap. *Explicitly confirmed:* DreamSpring, Accion Opportunity Fund, Working Solutions, Beneficial State Bank, MEDA, JFLA, LiftFund, Neighborhood Trust FCU, LES People's FCU, Mission Asset Fund. *Likely but unverified:* MoFi, Wind River Development Fund, Accompany Capital, Grameen, Ascendus, Renaissance, Brooklyn Coop, Pan American, PeopleFund, BCL, Communities Unlimited.
- **Bureau pull/report data is almost entirely unpublished** for these lenders — most cells are unverified. Known reporters: **Grameen America** (Experian/Equifax) and **Mission Asset Fund** (all 3 consumer bureaus); DreamSpring's Credit Builder product reports to build credit.
- **WebFetch was blocked (HTTP 403) on nearly every lender and government page.** Findings rest on search-result snippets cross-referenced with reputable third parties (CDFI Fund, OFN, Inclusiv, Native CDFI Network, CDFI Coalition, NerdWallet, Bankrate, LendingTree, NYC SBS) and are flagged where unverified.
- **The "Community Capital Wyoming" entity appears to be a search-engine fabrication** — no verifiable record exists.
- **Not legal or financial advice.** Confirm current eligibility directly with each lender and a qualified advisor.
